# Where to see Gessi taps in Hong Kong

COLOURLIVING presents Gessi bathroom taps at its flagship at **333 Lockhart Road, Wan Chai**. You can view collections, compare finishes and specify pieces with a specialist. Book a showroom visit; delivery is available within Hong Kong only.

Gessi is an Italian house known for sculptural basin mixers, showers and accessories. In Hong Kong, high-end taps are rarely a one-click purchase: concealed valves, PVD colours and spout reach have to match a real vanity and a real bathroom. That is why the useful search is not a random product grid — it is a place to **see Gessi in person** and leave with a specification the contractor can install.

## Where to buy Gessi taps in Hong Kong?

If you are searching **Gessi Hong Kong** or **Gessi tap Hong Kong**, start at COLOURLIVING’s [Gessi collection](https://colourliving.shop/collections/gessi). The page is the catalogue of what is currently presented online. The showroom is where you confirm what is on display, which finishes are available to order, and whether a piece is a display unit.

We do not treat the website as a substitute for the visit. Taps in black metal PVD, copper brushed PVD or nickel read very differently under showroom light than on a phone screen. Specialists at Wan Chai help you match Gessi to basins, concealed parts and project timelines.

COLOURLIVING is a House of Brands, not a DIY plumbing counter. If you need an installer, bring drawings or photos; we specify. We do not claim to be the only place in Hong Kong that has ever sold Gessi. We do say: this is where you can **see and specify** Gessi bathroom fittings with our team.

## What Gessi collections can I view?

Ranges rotate with display and incoming stock. Online you will typically find basin mixers and accessories from families such as [Rilievo](https://colourliving.shop/products/gessi-rilievo-59003-706-high-basin-mixer-with-waste-in-black-metal-pvd), Inciso, Venti20, Cono and Emporio — always check the live [Gessi collection](https://colourliving.shop/collections/gessi) rather than an old brochure.

Two habits save a failed renovation:

1. Specify **visible tap and concealed body** together. A beautiful spout with the wrong in-wall valve is an expensive delay.  
2. Lock **finish** across the room (tap, handshower, hooks). Gessi PVD colours are part of the design, not an afterthought.

For a wider European edit — not only Gessi — see [bathroom faucets](https://colourliving.shop/collections/bathroom-faucet) at COLOURLIVING, including Dornbracht and Fantini.

Read [About COLOURLIVING](https://colourliving.shop/pages/about-us) for how a visit works, then book. Walk-ins are possible; appointments get you a dedicated specialist.

## How do I specify Gessi for a Hong Kong bathroom?

Hong Kong bathrooms are often tighter than European lookbooks. When you search **Gessi 龍頭** as a homeowner or interior designer, bring three facts to Wan Chai:

- **Mounting:** deck-mounted, wall-mounted, or free-standing for a bath.  
- **Water system:** mains pressure and whether you need a thermostatic shower.  
- **Finish and maintenance:** chrome is honest in humid rooms; PVD colours need the care notes Gessi publishes — we will walk you through them in store.

Lead times on European fittings are normal. Display pieces may not be the sellable carton. Confirm condition, order time and [Hong Kong delivery](https://colourliving.shop/pages/about-us) before you pay. We do not ship outside Hong Kong.

Interior designers and project clients should say so when booking (FF&E, hospitality, residential). Trade process lives with the same Wan Chai team, not a separate anonymous checkout.
